Test Instructions

  1. Open the main application (index.html)
  2. Load a project with multiple tracks
  3. Pause the playback
  4. Touch and hold on the waveform canvas for 450ms at position A
  5. Verify that marker A appears with pulse animation
  6. Touch and hold on the waveform canvas for 450ms at position B
  7. Verify that marker B appears and loop region is shown
  8. Verify that loop toggle button appears
  9. Click the loop toggle button to enable loop
  10. Start playback and verify it loops between A and B
  11. Verify all tracks stay synchronized during loop
  12. Test mute/solo during loop to verify states are preserved
  13. Reload the page and verify loop state is persisted
